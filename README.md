# Hi, I'm Robert 👋

**Technical Writer · Hardware Documentation Specialist**

I spend my days turning complex hardware into documentation people can actually use. Over 9 years of end-to-end ownership, I've produced **900+ user manuals** across monitors, projectors, and large-format displays — and built the infrastructure that made that scale possible as a team of one.

## What I do

- 📄 **Documentation at scale** — 100+ guides per year, sustained for 9 years, with consistent style and structure across every product line
- 🏗️ **Documentation infrastructure** — designed and solo-built a searchable knowledge platform on MediaWiki (Bootstrap, CSS, JavaScript), meeting **WCAG 2.1 AA** with keyboard, NVDA, VoiceOver, and zoom testing verified
- 🧩 **Systems thinking** — reusable template architecture, style guides, SOPs, and a controlled-vocabulary system that cut terminology drift, reduced translation costs, and trimmed support friction
- 🤖 **Practical AI tooling** — Python proof-of-concepts for summarization, FAQ generation, and template population, built to remove repetitive tasks without replacing writer judgment
